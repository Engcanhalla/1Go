문제1주어진 글 다음에 이어질 글의 순서로 가장 적절한 것은?4점
Suppose you have been working on the solution to a challenging technological problem for many years.
(A) This does not mean that you must narrow down your interest to one specific topic. On the contrary, breadth of experience is the key to creativity. If your experience is limited to a narrow range of topics, then the scope of your imagination will likewise be narrow, which would be harmful to creativity. (B) Such "eureka moments" when great insights lead to discovery or invention often take place at unexpected times. To enhance spontaneous creativity, therefore, one must pay attention to the things that could potentially lead to valuable outcomes. (C) Let's also suppose that you have been paying attention to whatever else you have encountered that might be related to the problem. In that case, chances are that creative solutions to the problem could suddenly pop up in your mind when you are relaxed.
정답 ▸ 4번 · givenSentence에서 오랫동안 문제 해결에 몰두해 온 상황을 제시한 뒤, (C)에서 관련 경험에 주의를 기울였다면 이완 상태에서 창의적 해결책이 떠오를 수 있다고 이어지고, (B)에서 이런 '유레카 순간'이 예상치 못한 때 일어나므로 잠재적으로 가치 있는 것들에 주의를 기울여야 한다고 연결되며, (A)에서 관심 범위를 좁히지 말고 폭넓은 경험이 창의성의 핵심이라는 결론으로 마무리되므로 (C)-(B)-(A) 순서가 자연스럽다.

② 패러프레이징(para) — 아래 지문을 참고하여 푸시오
Suppose you have been working on the solution to a challenging technological problem for many years. Let's also suppose that you have been paying attention to whatever else you have encountered that might be related to the problem. In that case, chances are that creative solutions to the problem could suddenly pop up in your mind when you are relaxed. Such "eureka moments" when great insights lead to discovery or invention often take place at unexpected times. If your experience is limited to a narrow range of topics, then the scope of your imagination will likewise be narrow, which would be harmful to creativity.
