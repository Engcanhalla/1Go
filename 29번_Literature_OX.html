문제1주어진 글 다음에 이어질 글의 순서로 가장 적절한 것은?4점
Literature, in its essence, is a testament to the enduring power of human creativity and expression.
(A) Literature allows us to see ourselves reflected in the pages of a book, to empathize with characters different from ourselves, and to gain a deeper understanding of the human condition. (B) It transcends time and space, connecting us with the thoughts and emotions of people from all walks of life, across centuries and cultures. Despite the rise of new technologies and the ever-changing landscape of media, literature has retained its relevance and significance as a timeless art form. (C) One reason for literature's enduring power is its ability to capture and convey the complexities of the human experience. Through the written word, authors have the unique ability to explore the depths of human emotion, the nuances of relationships, and the challenges and triumphs of the human spirit.
정답 ▸ 5번 · 주어진 글의 Literature를 It으로 받아 시간과 공간을 초월한다고 설명하는 (B)가 먼저 오고, 그 지속적 힘(enduring power)의 이유를 제시하는 (C)가 이어지며, 그 결과 독자가 얻는 바(공감과 이해)를 마무리하는 (A)가 마지막에 온다.
